📊 EVALUATION METRICS EXPLANATION:

   MAE (Mean Absolute Error):
   • Average absolute difference between predicted and actual values
   • Interpretation: On average, predictions are off by 334.10 units
   • Lower is better

   RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error):
   • Penalizes larger errors more heavily than MAE
   • Interpretation: Typical prediction error is 816.27 units
   • Lower is better

   MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error):
   • Percentage-based error metric
   • Interpretation: Average error is 9.45% of actual value
   • Lower is better; < 10% is excellent, < 20% is good

   R² (Coefficient of Determination):
   • Proportion of variance explained by the model (0 to 1)
   • Interpretation: Model explains 96.04% of the variance
   • Higher is better; > 0.7 is excellent
