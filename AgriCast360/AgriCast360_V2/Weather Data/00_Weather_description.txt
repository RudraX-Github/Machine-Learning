🌦️ Weather Dataset Column Descriptions
Column				Description								Unit
clouds			Cloud cover percentage								%
datetime		Date of observation								Date (DD-MM-YYYY)
dewpt			Dew point temperature								°C
dhi			Diffuse Horizontal Irradiance (solar radiation scattered by atmosphere)		W/m²
dni			Direct Normal Irradiance (solar radiation directly from the sun)		W/m²
ghi			Global Horizontal Irradiance (total solar radiation on horizontal surface)	W/m²
max_dhi			Maximum DHI recorded during the day						W/m²
max_dni			Maximum DNI recorded during the day						W/m²
max_ghi			Maximum GHI recorded during the day						W/m²
max_temp		Maximum temperature recorded							°C
max_temp_ts		Timestamp when maximum temperature occurred					Unix time (seconds)
max_uv			Maximum UV index								Index (dimensionless)
max_wind_dir		Wind direction at maximum wind speed						Degrees (° from North)
max_wind_spd		Maximum wind speed recorded							m/s
max_wind_spd_ts		Timestamp when maximum wind speed occurred					Unix time (seconds)
min_temp		Minimum temperature recorded							°C
min_temp_ts		Timestamp when minimum temperature occurred					Unix time (seconds)
precip			Precipitation amount								mm
precip_gpm		Precipitation rate (from GPM satellite or model)				mm/hr
pres			Atmospheric pressure at station level						hPa
revision_status		Data quality flag (e.g., final, provisional)					Text
rh			Relative humidity								%
slp			Sea-level pressure								hPa
snow			Snowfall amount									mm
snow_depth		Snow depth									mm
solar_rad		Solar radiation									W/m²
t_dhi			Timestamp for DHI measurement							Unix time (seconds)
t_dni			Timestamp for DNI measurement							Unix time (seconds)
t_ghi			Timestamp for GHI measurement							Unix time (seconds)
t_solar_rad		Timestamp for solar radiation measurement					Unix time (seconds)
temp			Air temperature									°C
ts			General timestamp for record							Unix time (seconds)
wind_dir		Average wind direction								Degrees (° from North)
wind_gust_spd		Wind gust speed (short-term peak)						m/s
wind_spd		Average wind speed								m/s
market_name		Market/location name								Text
query_lat		Latitude of market location							Decimal degrees
query_lon		Longitude of market location							Decimal degrees
