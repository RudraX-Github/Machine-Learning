Feature Engineering Methodology

1. Agricultural Domain Knowledge

  • Growing Degree Days (GDD): Accumulated heat units critical for crop development stages.
  • Drought Stress: Consecutive dry days and rainfall deficit impact yield and thus supply.
  • Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD): Indicates atmospheric demand for water, affecting crop water stress.
  • Extreme Weather Events: Heatwaves and heavy rainfall directly impact crop quality and market supply.

2. Financial Market Techniques

  • Technical Indicators: RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands borrowed from financial analysis.
  • Momentum Features: Price velocity and acceleration capture market dynamics.
  • Cross-Market Arbitrage: Price spreads indicate market inefficiencies and transportation costs.

3. Time Series Engineering

  • Cyclical Encoding: Sin/Cos transformation preserves seasonal patterns.
  • Multi-Window Rolling Features: Capture both short-term and long-term trends.
  • Lag Features: Autoregressive patterns from 1 to 30 days.
